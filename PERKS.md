# Waybackers Personal Perks 0.3.0

22 starting perks replace the character pack starting perks. Clover also retains Dublin Rooftops on tier 3. Hit chance bonuses are percentage points; damage, suppression and AP percentages are relative.

## Quotations

Literary extracts are sourced below. Other quotations are original lines written for these fictional characters, not attributed to real authors. In-game descriptions omit source footnotes. Clover retains the user-provided lyrics and the approved adaptation "She took a steady aim.".

## Initial perks

### Clover — Sniper's Promise / Обещание снайпера

> The night was icy cold I stood along  
> I was waiting for an army foot patrol  
> And when at last they came into my site  
> I squeezed the trigger of my armalite

Primary weapons excluding sniper rifles: +15 percentage points to hit chance, minimum 30%, before movement. Movement removes both effects until the next turn.

Source: user-provided excerpt, preserved exactly as approved.

### Clover, tier 3 — Dublin Rooftops / Крыши Дублина

> She took a steady aim.

After learning this perk, sniper rifles gain +10 percentage points to hit chance, minimum 50%, before movement. Movement removes both effects until the next turn. Does not stack with Sniper's Promise; other tier 3 choices are preserved.

Source: approved adaptation of Liam O'Flaherty's *The Sniper*, not a verbatim quotation. [Text, page 72](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED451116.pdf#page=72).

### Ash — Ashwake Vendetta / Месть Эшвейка

> I buried my family. I did not bury the debt.

+15 percentage points to hit chance and +10% direct damage against pirates.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Bulk — Extra Ballistic Protection / Лишняя баллистическая защита

> It's not extra weight. It's extra ballistic protection.

+10% squad member hit points. With a machine gun equipped, incoming suppression is reduced by 15%.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Caleb — Hold the Line / Держать рубеж

> The readiness is all.

While in cover and before moving this turn: +10 percentage points to primary weapon hit chance and 20% less incoming suppression.

Source: [Shakespeare, Hamlet, V.2](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1524/pg1524-images.html).

### Camila — Contract Kill / Работа по контракту

> One round. One invoice.

The first sniper attack each turn gains +10 percentage points to hit chance and +15% direct damage. A miss also spends the bonus; movement does not.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Creed — Old Drill / Старая выучка

> This uniform has outlived better men than you.

+10 discipline. The first primary weapon attack each turn costs 10% fewer action points.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Crow — Frontier Watch / Пограничный дозор

> Out here, the horizon is the only warning you get.

+1 vision range. Deploying the squad costs 10% fewer supplies.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Darius — First Through the Breach / Первым в пролом

> Once more unto the breach, dear friends, once more.

The first attack each turn, if its target is within 3 tiles, deals +20% direct damage and +25% suppression.

Source: [Shakespeare, Henry V, III.1](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1521/1521-h/1521-h.htm).

### Don — Protective Detail / Группа прикрытия

> The client goes home. That's the job.

Other friendly infantry squads within 2 tiles take 10% less direct damage. Don does not receive his own protection; identical auras do not stack.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Lucky — Long Haul / Дальний рейс

> Bad road. Good driver. We'll get there.

Vehicle movement through costly terrain uses 20% fewer action points, never less than the normal terrain cost.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Luo — By the Book / По инструкции

> Orders first. Opinions later.

While another friendly infantry squad is within 3 tiles: +10 discipline and +5 percentage points to primary weapon hit chance.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Lynx — Silent Observer / Тихий наблюдатель

> The less they see, the more I know.

+1 camouflage and +1 detection range. Attacks from concealment gain +10 percentage points to hit chance.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Marcus — Room Service / Обслуживание номеров

> Knock once. Clear twice.

Primary weapon attacks within 3 tiles gain +10 percentage points to hit chance and cost 10% fewer action points.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Markov — Measured Bursts / Размеренные очереди

> Short bursts. Long day.

Machine guns deal +50% suppression. Before moving this turn, machine gun attacks gain +10 percentage points to hit chance.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Null — Settle the Account / Закрыть счёт

> Nothing will come of nothing.

Deal +15% direct damage to targets below 50% hit points. The first kill each turn refunds 20 action points.

Source: [Shakespeare, King Lear, I.1](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1532).

### Rook — Combat Driver / Боевой водитель

> Cargo doesn't care who's shooting.

Vehicle turns and reversing cost 25% fewer action points. The first shot after a turn this turn gains +10 percentage points to hit chance.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Sigrid — Still Breathing / Ещё дышит

> If you can complain, you can survive.

Retains First Aid healing for own squad and friendly squad leaders, including its normal use limits. Costs 25% fewer action points. The treated unit takes 20% less suppression until the end of its next turn.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Talia — My Way / По-своему

> I have my own way of following orders.

After the first attack each turn, the next 2 movement tiles cost 25% fewer action points. Unused movement expires at the end of the turn.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Track — Steel Routine / Стальная привычка

> Steel remembers what men forget.

Before moving this turn, vehicle guns gain +10 percentage points to hit chance and +15% armor penetration.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Vale — Live to Walk Away / Выжить и уйти

> The better part of valour is discretion.

After taking direct damage, the next 2 movement tiles cost 25% fewer action points, through the end of the next own turn. Further hits refresh this benefit without stacking.

Source: [Shakespeare, Henry IV Part 1, V.4](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1516).

### Viper — Cobra's Step / Шаг кобры

> Precision is not patience. Precision is control.

Walkers spend 2 fewer action points per movement tile. The first attack after moving each turn gains +10 percentage points to hit chance.

Source: original character line written for this mod.

### Voss — Let It Burn / Пусть горит

> Hell is empty, and all the devils are here.

Infantry flamethrower attacks deal +20% direct damage and +25% suppression. Does not alter damage from fire left on the ground.

Source: [Shakespeare, The Tempest, I.2](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/23042).
